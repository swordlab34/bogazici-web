Smile design

Content last reviewed: 1 November 2026 Medically reviewed · Dt. Edanur Alkan

What is smile design?

Smile design is not a single treatment but a planning process carried out before an aesthetic change. The colour, shape and length of the teeth, the gum line and their harmony with the lips are assessed together; which treatments are needed is decided according to this plan.

How is the planning done?

Photographs of the face and smile are taken, and a digital impression is made with an intraoral scanner. The relationship of the teeth to the proportions of the face is studied from these records. Your expectations and what you would like to change are discussed at this stage.

Preview

Where suitable, the shape of the planned teeth can be seen in advance through a digital design or a temporary trial in the mouth. A preview gives an idea; the final result may differ depending on the condition of the teeth and tissues.

What can the plan include?

  • Teeth whitening
  • Composite bonding
  • Veneers or crowns
  • Orthodontics
  • Gum contouring

If there is decay or gum disease, the plan starts with treating these. Sometimes small changes are enough; a plan does not always mean covering many teeth.
