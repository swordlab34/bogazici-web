Hollywood smile

Content last reviewed: 1 November 2026 Medically reviewed · Dt. Edanur Alkan

What does Hollywood smile mean?

"Hollywood smile" is not the name of a medical treatment. It is a general name for aesthetic work in which all the teeth visible when smiling are planned together. The plan often includes veneers or crowns; sometimes whitening, gum contouring or orthodontics are part of it too.

The same plan does not suit everyone

A good plan is made for the individual, according to the proportions of the face, the lip line, the gum level and the current condition of the teeth. Very white, uniform teeth may not look natural on everyone; this is why colour and shape are chosen together.

Oral health first

If there is decay, gum disease or teeth that need root canal treatment, these are treated before any aesthetic work. Veneers or crowns placed on an unhealthy foundation may not last as long.

Irreversible steps

For veneers and crowns, some tooth tissue is removed; this cannot be undone. Veneers and crowns are restorations that may need to be renewed over time. For this reason, how many teeth will be covered, which material will be chosen and the alternatives should be discussed openly at the examination.
