Medical Disclaimer

Last updated · 4 July 2026

Not medical advice

The content of this website is provided for general information about the clinic and the treatments it offers. It is not medical or dental advice, it is not a diagnosis, and it must not be used as a basis for starting, changing or stopping any treatment. No content on this site creates a dentist-patient relationship.

Examination comes first

Every mouth is different. A responsible treatment decision requires a clinical examination and, in most cases, radiographic imaging. Assessments we provide remotely, based on photographs or scans you share, are preliminary by nature and are confirmed, adjusted or revised at the examination in Istanbul before any treatment begins.

Results vary

Photographs and descriptions of treatments on this site illustrate what modern dentistry can achieve; they are not a promise of an identical outcome. Results depend on your oral health, bone structure, general health, healing response and aftercare. Where we describe expected outcomes, survival rates or timelines, these reflect published clinical evidence and our experience, not a guarantee for an individual case.

Risks and complications

All dental and surgical procedures carry risks. Before any treatment you will be informed of the relevant risks, alternatives and aftercare requirements, and your written informed consent will be obtained. Surgical procedures at the clinic are covered by the mandatory complication insurance required by Turkish law.

Emergencies

This website and our WhatsApp line are not emergency services. If you have severe pain, uncontrolled bleeding, facial swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or any other urgent condition, contact your local emergency services or the nearest emergency department immediately.

Questions

If anything on this site is unclear, ask us before you decide. We would rather answer one more question than have you decide on incomplete information.
