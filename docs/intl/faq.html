Frequently asked questions

Last updated · 6 July 2026

Getting started

How do I begin as an international patient?

Write to our WhatsApp line at any hour; your message is always answered. In the first conversation we look at your wish and your general situation, then map out a route that suits you. The exact treatment plan is shared, as the law requires, after a clinical examination at the practice.

Which languages can I use?

Our international patient unit is staffed by multilingual coordinators. The person who answers your first message stays with you to the last.

Can I get a treatment plan and price in advance?

Turkish health regulation requires that the treatment plan and price be given individually, after a clinical examination at the practice. General information can be shared beforehand, but the exact plan rests on examining your mouth.

The clinic and your guarantees

Is the clinic licensed?

Yes. A fully licensed oral and dental health polyclinic in Istanbul, inspected and accountable under Turkish health law. Our certificates are shown on the home page.

Are surgical procedures insured?

Surgical procedures are covered by mandatory complication insurance, as required by law.

Does the dentist who plans my treatment perform it?

Yes. Each discipline is led by its own specialist; the dentist who plans your treatment performs it and answers for it afterwards.

Process and technology

Will impression trays be used?

No. Your mouth is measured with intraoral 3D scanners; no impression trays. The treatment plan is prepared on screen.

Where are the restorations made?

Veneers, crowns and bridges are made in our own laboratory under this roof. Zirconia crowns, veneers and smile design restorations are all prepared in this laboratory too. Milling machines, sintering furnaces and a ceramics bench are all here; nothing is sent out.

I am a nervous patient. What are my options?

Everything from gentle local numbing to sedation and full general anaesthesia is run and monitored by a dedicated anaesthesia team. Which method suits you is decided by the dentist who examines you.

How long does treatment take?

The duration depends on the treatment and the condition of your mouth. The exact duration is set out in your personal plan after the examination and panoramic X-ray at the clinic. In implant treatment, the implant takes a few months to fuse with the bone; the restoration on top is made after that.

How many times will I need to come to the clinic?

The number of visits depends on the type of treatment and the condition of your mouth. After the examination, the plan prepared for you explains which stage is done at which visit.

Are X-rays and CBCT scans taken at the clinic?

Panoramic X-rays are taken at the clinic; a CBCT scan is requested where needed for planning.

How is the shade of my restoration chosen?

The patient, the dentist and the dental technician choose the shade together; the patient makes the final decision.

How do I get to the clinic by public transport?

Mahmutbey metro station is about a 15-minute walk from the clinic and Göztepe Mahallesi (M7) station about 20 minutes; a shuttle runs from both stations to the complex where the clinic is located. The HT3 and HT6 buses from Kocatepe metro station pass in front of the clinic. The clinic has its own parking.

Can I stay with my child during the examination?

The dentist decides whether a parent stays with the child during the examination.

This page is for information only. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by your dentist after clinical examination.
