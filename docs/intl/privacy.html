What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, WhatsApp profile name, email address.
  • Your messages to us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
  • Health information you choose to send before your visit: description of your dental condition, photos, X-rays, previous treatment.
  • Booking details: appointment dates, travel dates, treatment plan and price offered.
  • If you become our patient: the examination and treatment records Turkish health law requires us to keep.
  • If you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad: the ad you clicked, as reported by Meta.

We do not use cookies or tracking on our website.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Answering your enquiry, giving you information and a price estimate, booking your appointment: Art. 6(1)(b), steps you asked us to take before a contract.
  • Assessing your dental condition from the information you send before your visit: Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 9(2)(a), your explicit consent for health data.
  • Providing and recording your treatment: Art. 6(1)(b), Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 9(2)(h), health care by professionals bound by confidentiality.
  • Keeping records required by Turkish health law: Art. 6(1)(c), legal obligation.
  • Following up on your enquiry if you have not replied: Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in answering patients who contacted us; you can tell us to stop at any time.

We do not send you marketing messages unless you agree to receive them. We do not sell your data.

Your health data and your consent

Sending us photos, X-rays or details about your teeth is optional. If you send them, you give us your explicit consent to use them to assess your case and prepare a treatment plan. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

Replies prepared with an AI assistant

Some of our replies on WhatsApp are written by an AI assistant that works on our behalf, and our patient coordinators see the conversation. The assistant does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects; a dentist decides your treatment. You can ask to speak to a member of our team at any time.

Who we share your data with

Our staff see your data only as needed to help you. We also use these service providers, who process your data on our instructions:

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. (WhatsApp Business): carries our WhatsApp messages with you; EU and USA.
  • Anthropic PBC: AI assistant that drafts replies to your messages; USA.
  • netcup GmbH: hosts our patient management system; Germany.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH: encrypted backups; Finland.
  • Verimor Telekomünikasyon A.Ş.: our telephone system; Türkiye.
  • Zoho Corporation: our email.

We share data with public authorities only where the law requires it. When Meta delivers WhatsApp messages, its own terms and privacy policy also apply.

Transfers outside the UK and the EU

We are based in Türkiye, so your data is processed in Türkiye, which does not have an adequacy decision from the EU or the UK. When you contact us, the transfer is necessary to answer your request and to take the steps you ask for before treatment (Art. 49(1)(b)). Transfers to our service providers in the USA are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, for UK data, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to treatment: 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Patient and treatment records: 20 years after your treatment ends, as Turkish health law requires, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Some records must be kept by law even if you ask us to delete them; we will tell you if this applies. We answer within one month.

To use your rights, write to management@bogaziciadsp.com or to our address above.

Complaints

You can complain to us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the authority in the country where you live or work; in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu).

BOĞAZİÇİ ÖZEL AĞIZ VE DİŞ SAĞLIK HİZMETLERİ LTD. ŞTİ.
Personal Data Protection Information Notice: Supplementary Section
“Online Smile Preview (Smile Simulation)” Service

1. Data controller: Boğaziçi Özel Ağız ve Diş Sağlık Hizmetleri Ltd. Şti., Göztepe Mah. Batışehir Cad. G3 Blok No:6G (Batışehir Sitesi, Mahmutbey), 34218 Bağcılar / İstanbul. Contact: info@bogaziciadsp.com, +90 212 706 96 09.

2. What the service is: If you request it, an AI-generated representative smile image is prepared from the smile photo you send, according to the treatment style you choose (natural veneers, Hollywood smile, etc.). The image is checked by our dentist and then sent to you via WhatsApp. The image is for illustration only; it does not guarantee a treatment result. The definitive treatment plan is set by your dentist after an examination.

3. Personal data processed: identity and contact (name and surname if you provided them, WhatsApp number, language of correspondence); visual data (photo of the mouth and face area and the representative image produced); information that may qualify as health data (the condition of the teeth and gums visible in the photo, treatment interest and complaints shared in the correspondence); transaction records (consent and age declaration, times of request, approval and sending).

4. Purposes of processing: preparing the representative smile image, having it reviewed by a dentist and sending it to you; contacting you for examination and treatment planning if you request it; service quality and security; legal obligations.

5. Legal basis: Because the photo and the image produced may qualify as health data, they are processed on the basis of your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 6). If you do not give explicit consent, you cannot use this service; our clinical services are not affected. Contact and record data are also processed to provide the service you requested (Art. 5/2-c) and on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f).

6. Transfer abroad: To produce the image, the photo and your style preference are transferred to the servers of the AI service provider OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States of America). Your name and phone number are not sent. Under the service provider's contractual terms, this data is not used for model training. Messaging is carried out via WhatsApp Business on the infrastructure of Meta Platforms (Ireland/USA). There is no adequacy decision of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board regarding the USA; this transfer is made on the basis of the explicit consent you give after being informed of the possible risks (KVKK Art. 9/6-a). If you do not give consent, the image cannot be prepared.

7. Retention period: The photo and the image produced are deleted automatically after 30 days. If an examination and treatment begin, treatment records are kept for the periods required by health legislation.

8. Age limit: The service is offered only to persons aged 18 or over.

9. Your rights: Your rights under KVKK Art. 11 (to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, rectification, erasure, to learn the third parties it was transferred to, to object, and compensation for damages). You can withdraw your explicit consent at any time; in that case the photo and the image are deleted. Applications: info@bogaziciadsp.com or our postal address; concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
